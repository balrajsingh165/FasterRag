# Expense Reimbursement

Expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of an approved claim. Claims must be filed
within 60 days of the expenditure; claims filed later are rejected without appeal.

Receipts are required for every item above 25 units of local currency. Travel
booked outside the approved booking tool is reimbursed at 80 percent of the amount
paid, to discourage out-of-policy booking.
