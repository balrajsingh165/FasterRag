# Onboarding

A new joiner's equipment is dispatched to arrive two working days before the start
date. Accounts are provisioned on the start date itself and never earlier, so that
an offer withdrawn before day one leaves no active credentials.

The probation period is six months. A probation review at three months is mandatory
and its outcome is recorded in the people portal; a missed review extends probation
by the number of days it was late.
