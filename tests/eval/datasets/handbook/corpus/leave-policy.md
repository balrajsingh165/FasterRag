# Leave Policy

Employees accrue 1.75 days of paid annual leave for each completed calendar month
of service, to a maximum of 21 days per year. Unused leave above 5 days is
forfeited at the end of the leave year and is not paid out on departure.

Leave requests must be submitted at least 14 calendar days in advance through the
people portal. Requests spanning a quarter-end close require the approval of the
finance director in addition to the line manager.
