# System access — Administrator

Applies to the **Administrator** role. Access is granted at onboarding and reviewed every
60 days.

## Default grants

Administrators receive every system in the estate. Production database access is **read and write**. Elevated privileges
require **two directors** and are time-boxed to 2 hours.

## Offboarding

Access is revoked within **1 hours** of the leaving date. Shared credentials the
Administrator held are rotated within 1 hours.

## Exceptions

A standing exception requires sign-off from the CTO and is re-reviewed every 30 days.
