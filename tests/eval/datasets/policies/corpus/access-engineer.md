# System access — Engineer

Applies to the **Engineer** role. Access is granted at onboarding and reviewed every
90 days.

## Default grants

Engineers receive the source repository, the staging cluster, and the incident channel. Production database access is **read-only**. Elevated privileges
require **a peer reviewer and the on-call lead** and are time-boxed to 8 hours.

## Offboarding

Access is revoked within **4 hours** of the leaving date. Shared credentials the
Engineer held are rotated within 12 hours.

## Exceptions

A standing exception requires sign-off from the engineering director and is re-reviewed every 180 days.
