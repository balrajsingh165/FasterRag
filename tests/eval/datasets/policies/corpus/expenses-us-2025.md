# Travel expenses — the United States, FY2025

Applies to employees based in the United States for the financial year 2025.

## Daily allowances

The meal allowance is **$59 per day**. The incidental allowance is **$12 per day**.
Accommodation is reimbursed up to **$185 per night** in standard cities and **$275
per night** in New York.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at **67 cents per mile** for the first
15,000 miles in a claim period, and at 41 cents thereafter.

## Submission

Claims must be filed within 60 days of the expenditure. Receipts are required above
$40. Claims filed after the window require director approval and are settled in the
following 2026 cycle.
