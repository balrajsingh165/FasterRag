# Travel expenses — the United Kingdom, FY2026

Applies to employees based in the United Kingdom for the financial year 2026.

## Daily allowances

The meal allowance is **£41 per day**. The incidental allowance is **£7 per day**.
Accommodation is reimbursed up to **£155 per night** in standard cities and **£230
per night** in London.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at **47p per mile** for the first
10,000 miles in a claim period, and at 27p thereafter.

## Submission

Claims must be filed within 45 days of the expenditure. Receipts are required above
£30. Claims filed after the window require director approval and are settled in the
following 2027 cycle.
