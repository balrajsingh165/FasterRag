# System access — Contractor

Applies to the **Contractor** role. Access is granted at onboarding and reviewed every
30 days.

## Default grants

Contractors receive the source repository and the staging cluster. Production database access is **never granted**. Elevated privileges
require **the engagement owner** and are time-boxed to 4 hours.

## Offboarding

Access is revoked within **1 hours** of the leaving date. Shared credentials the
Contractor held are rotated within 2 hours.

## Exceptions

A standing exception requires sign-off from the security lead and is re-reviewed every 30 days.
