# System access — Analyst

Applies to the **Analyst** role. Access is granted at onboarding and reviewed every
120 days.

## Default grants

Analysts receive the reporting warehouse and the metrics dashboards. Production database access is **read-only on replicas**. Elevated privileges
require **the data lead** and are time-boxed to 12 hours.

## Offboarding

Access is revoked within **8 hours** of the leaving date. Shared credentials the
Analyst held are rotated within 24 hours.

## Exceptions

A standing exception requires sign-off from the data director and is re-reviewed every 90 days.
