# Travel expenses — the United Kingdom, FY2025

Applies to employees based in the United Kingdom for the financial year 2025.

## Daily allowances

The meal allowance is **£38 per day**. The incidental allowance is **£6 per day**.
Accommodation is reimbursed up to **£140 per night** in standard cities and **£210
per night** in London.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at **45p per mile** for the first
10,000 miles in a claim period, and at 25p thereafter.

## Submission

Claims must be filed within 30 days of the expenditure. Receipts are required above
£25. Claims filed after the window require director approval and are settled in the
following 2026 cycle.
