# Travel expenses — the United States, FY2026

Applies to employees based in the United States for the financial year 2026.

## Daily allowances

The meal allowance is **$64 per day**. The incidental allowance is **$14 per day**.
Accommodation is reimbursed up to **$199 per night** in standard cities and **$295
per night** in New York.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at **70 cents per mile** for the first
15,000 miles in a claim period, and at 44 cents thereafter.

## Submission

Claims must be filed within 90 days of the expenditure. Receipts are required above
$45. Claims filed after the window require director approval and are settled in the
following 2027 cycle.
