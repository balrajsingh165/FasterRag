# Parental leave — the United Kingdom, FY2026

Applies to employees based in the United Kingdom for the financial year 2026.

## Entitlement

Primary carers receive **30 weeks** of paid parental leave. Secondary carers receive
**6 weeks**. Leave must begin within 18 months of the birth or placement.

## Continuous service

Eligibility requires **18 months** of continuous service at the start of the leave.
Employees below that threshold receive the statutory the United Kingdom minimum instead.

## Return to work

A phased return over **8 weeks** at reduced hours is available on request. Notice of the
intended return date is required 6 weeks in advance.
