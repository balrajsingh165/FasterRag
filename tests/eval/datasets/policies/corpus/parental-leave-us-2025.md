# Parental leave — the United States, FY2025

Applies to employees based in the United States for the financial year 2025.

## Entitlement

Primary carers receive **16 weeks** of paid parental leave. Secondary carers receive
**8 weeks**. Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or placement.

## Continuous service

Eligibility requires **12 months** of continuous service at the start of the leave.
Employees below that threshold receive the statutory the United States minimum instead.

## Return to work

A phased return over **4 weeks** at reduced hours is available on request. Notice of the
intended return date is required 4 weeks in advance.
